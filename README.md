# RXG-IV-Recomp
Bringing the GTA IV Xbox 360 experience to Android.

RXG IV Recomp is an Android-focused project based on XHYN IV Recomp https://github.com/XHYN-PH/XHYN-IV-recomp, aiming to deliver a smoother and more enjoyable Grand Theft Auto IV experience inspired by the original Xbox 360 version.

Main Goals

- 🔊 Audio Improvements: Work towards eliminating audio crackling, distortion, static, and sound glitches.
- ⚡ Performance Optimization: Improve frame stability, reduce stuttering, and optimize gameplay performance.
- 🛠️ Stability Improvements: Investigate crashes, bugs, and compatibility issues.
- 📱 Android Optimization: Improve compatibility and gameplay on a wider range of Android devices.Cause i also Has a Midrange Device🥲
